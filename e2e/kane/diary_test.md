# Log water and a custom food

## Sign in

Enter "alex" as the username and "Garden-2024!" as the password, then click "Sign In".
Verify the Diary opens on "Today" and the overview shows 0 kcal.

## Log water

In the Water card, click "+500 ml" and verify it shows "500 ml of 2000 ml".

## Create a custom food

Click "Foods" in the left menu and create a new custom food named "Overnight oats" with 350 calories per serving, then save it.
Verify "Overnight oats" is listed in Foods.

## Add it to breakfast

Click "Diary" in the left menu, click "Tap to add food" under Breakfast, find "Overnight oats" and add one serving.
Verify "Overnight oats" is listed under Breakfast with "350 kcal" on its line.
